---
title: Is my data used to train AI models?
description: No. Nothing is sent to Neato Ventures at all — and what changes if you bring your own cloud provider key.
---
**Neato Echo does not use your content for AI training, and it can't.**
Nothing you dictate, record, upload or type is ever sent to Neato Ventures.
There's no account, no server side and no analytics. With the default local
setup, your audio, transcripts and notes never leave your PC.

## What that covers

Everything you put through the product:

* the audio you record
* the transcripts that come back
* your notes
* prompts and conversations with the agent

All of it is processed and stored on your own machine.

## If you use your own cloud provider account

If you add a key under **Settings → Cloud Providers** and point transcription
or the language model at it, requests go directly from your PC to that
provider. Neato Echo isn't in the path, so **your provider agreement and
account settings** govern training there.

Most providers disable training on paid API traffic by default, but it's your
account and your agreement with them. If training policy is the reason you'd
consider a cloud provider, check the terms on the key you're using — or stay
local, which avoids the question entirely.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting a calendar change this?">
    No. A calendar connection reads your events from Google or Microsoft so
    Neato Echo can remind you about meetings. Nothing is sent to them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you read my transcripts?">
    We can't. They're on your PC and nowhere else. Support only sees what you
    choose to paste into an email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get this in writing for a vendor review?">
    The [privacy policy](https://echo.neatoventures.com/privacy.html) is the
    document to send, and [for your IT team](/help/privacy/for-your-it-team)
    summarizes what the app does and doesn't connect to. The source is open on
    GitHub if your reviewer wants to check for themselves.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
