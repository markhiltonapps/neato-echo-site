Neato Echo holds no data about you. There's no account, and Neato Ventures
operates no server that receives your audio, transcripts or notes. Everything
the app stores is on your own PC, which means every right the GDPR gives you
over that data is something you exercise directly, without asking anyone.

## Your rights and how to use each one

| Right                                             | How                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Access** — a copy of what is held               | It's all in `%APPDATA%\neato-echo`. Open the Notes view, or copy the folder — see [where your files live](/platform/where-your-files-live) |
| **Portability** — your content in a usable format | Export any note as Markdown, or turn on **Save notes as files** to mirror every note to a folder — see [export your notes](/help/notes/export-your-notes) |
| **Correction**                                    | Edit the note or transcript in the app                                                                  |
| **Erasure**                                       | Delete notes individually, use **Clear All Audio**, or **Reset app data** under **Settings → System** — see [what is stored, and for how long](/help/privacy/what-we-store-and-for-how-long) |
| **Restriction and objection**                     | Turn **Data Retention** off so nothing is saved, or don't connect the provider in question             |

Because nothing is held by Neato Ventures, there's no request to file and no
waiting period. Deleting the files deletes the data.

## Providers you choose to connect

Two optional features involve a third party, and each has its own privacy
policy that applies alongside the rights above:

* **Calendars** — connecting Google Calendar or a Microsoft calendar lets
  Neato Echo read your events. Google and Microsoft remain the controllers of
  that calendar data. Disconnecting in **Settings → Integrations** stops the
  sync and removes the app's access; you can also revoke it from your Google
  or Microsoft account settings.
* **Cloud providers** — if you add an API key under **Settings → Cloud
  Providers**, the audio or text you send goes to that provider under its own
  terms. Requests for access, deletion or objection about data they hold go to
  them.

If you'd rather nothing left your machine at all, leave both off — that's the
default. [Where your voice and text go](/help/privacy/where-your-data-goes)
explains the full picture.

## Training

Your content is never used to train AI models by Neato Ventures, because it
never reaches us. That's [its own
article](/help/privacy/is-my-data-used-to-train-ai) because it's the question
we're asked most.
