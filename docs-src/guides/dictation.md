title: How dictation works

Neato Echo listens when you press a key, transcribes what you say, and types it
wherever your cursor is. It works in any app: email, chat, a code editor, a
document. Nothing to copy, no window to switch to.

## How it works

1. **Press your hotkey.** That's **Ctrl+Win** by default.
2. **Speak.** The panel shows it's recording.
3. **Press it again to stop**, or hold the key throughout and release, if you've
   set it to [hold](/help/dictation/hold-or-tap).
4. **The text is transcribed and pasted** at your cursor.

Transcription happens on your PC using the Parakeet speech model. There is an
optional second stage, [cleanup](/help/dictation/cleanup), which tidies the words
before they land using the local language model. Cleanup is off by default in
Neato Echo, so what you say is what gets typed; turn it on if you'd rather your
dictation read more like writing.

## It isn't only dictation

The same voice, on a different shortcut, can carry out an instruction rather
than be typed out. If you're getting answers instead of your words, that's the
voice agent. [The four things your voice can start](/help/agent/voice-modes)
tells them apart.
